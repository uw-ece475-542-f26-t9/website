## Weekly Progress

- Completed soldering the prototype hardware and began testing the modules individually.
- Prepared and delivered the project status presentation, including an edited milestone demo video.
- Implemented the signal processing and ML pipeline, including signal cleaning, angle-based segmentation, feature extraction, Kalman filtering, and model-based warnings.
- Developed data collection and video-labeling tools. The pipeline passed 112 software tests, while model training on real participant data remains pending.

## Outcomes and Decisions

- Use seconds until the first unsuccessful repetition, identified from video, as the prediction target.
- Maintain a metronome pace of one second up and one second down during data collection and the demo. Target warning times are four and two seconds before the stop point.
- Use simulated recordings to test the pipeline and real participant recordings to train the models.
- Compare EMG features within matching forearm angle intervals, separating lifting and lowering.
- Evaluate predictions using leave-one-person-out validation, tracking missed warnings, false alarms, warning lead time, and prediction error.

## Next steps

- Complete individual module tests and verify the full system on the soldered prototype.
- Run a pilot recording to check sensor quality, video synchronization, and the labeling workflow.
- Collect EMG, IMU, and synchronized video data at the IMA using the agreed protocol.
- Label the recordings, train and compare the candidate models, and evaluate warning performance.
- Test the updated capture firmware on the XIAO and connect the selected warning output to haptic feedback.
