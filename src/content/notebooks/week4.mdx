## Weekly Progress

1. Conducted weighted bicep curl tests using the EMG sensor setup. Successfully captured muscle activity and observed signal changes consistent with fatigue as the exercise progressed.
2. Reviewed the placement and wiring of the components on the perfboard, including the ESP32, power connections, and supporting circuitry.
3. Designed and 3D-printed an initial enclosure prototype for the wearable device.

## Outcomes and Decisions

1. Decided to add a power switch between the battery and the ESP32 to turn the device on and off.
2. Completed the initial enclosure design and print. The printed prototype will be used to evaluate component fit, cable routing, and strap attachment.
3. Clarified the electrode configuration: the MyoWare sensor will remain on the biceps, with the MID and END electrodes attached directly beneath the board. Only the REF electrode requires an extension cable to reach a separate reference location. Power and signal wiring between the MyoWare and ESP32 is still required.
