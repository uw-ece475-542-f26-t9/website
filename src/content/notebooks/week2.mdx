## Outcomes and Decisions

- We established our main platform as the ESP32-S3.
- We decided on the MyoWare 2.0 as our EMG sensor. We have some concerns about the fixed gain and frequency ranges of the MyoWare, so we wanted to ensure we had it early in case we need to exchange parts. Integration with this sensor is expected to be much easier than other market options, so we are starting here.
- We chose the MPU6050 as our IMU.
- After the lecture on apps and integration, we decided to use Flutter to build our mobile app. Given the heterogenous nature of our team's hardware, this allows for a flexible development environment that we can compile for any platform. While developing for iOS with Swift and Xcode would have been a fun challenge, we deemed it too difficult given the limited number of Macs in our team.
